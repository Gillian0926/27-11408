> **轮次站点**：第 6 轮第 1 站（**#1/15**）；上一站 #15 环境 🌿（11-16），下一站 #2 乐观积极 ☀️（11-18）
> **素材库编号**：**#1 梦想 🌠**；素材库文件：《英语二/素材库/01-作文素材句库.md》
> **主题词速记**：dream / aspiration / vision / goal / what drives us forward / the fabric of dream / castle in the air（写作时轮换使用，避免整段只重复 `dream` 和 `good`）
> ⚠️ **辨析**：`dream` 作“梦想”讲可数，`dreams` 更常用于泛指；`dream of / about doing sth` 表示“梦想做某事”；`dream up` 是“虚构、想出”。`ingredient` 是可数名词，`the ingredient` 在此特指“那个要素”；`mediocrity` 不可数；`stream` 可数，`the stream of success` 用 `the` 表比喻中的特指。`change A to B` 与 `change A into B` 均可，前者强调结果/方向，后者强调转变过程；本句用 `to`，不要写成 `change A with B`。`nourish` 是及物动词，直接接宾语；`represent possibility` 中 `possibility` 作抽象概念，不加冠词；若说“一种可能”，则用 `a possibility`。`drag` 的过去分词是 `dragged`，常搭配 `be dragged down by...`；`push sb around` 是“随意摆布某人”，被动为 `be pushed around`；`lead` 的过去式、过去分词均为 `led`，`be led by` 是“被……引领”。
> **今日辨析主线**：**三句话分别解决“定义梦想 / 描述梦想作用 / 给出行动选择”三种表达任务**——
> ① 主句 `Dream is the ingredient that changes the hot water of mediocrity to the stream of success.`：**主系表 + `that` 定语从句 + 暗喻**，用于说明“梦想是改变平庸处境的关键要素”，适合第二段解释句；
> ② 补充句① `Dreams nourish our spirit, give us a vision of a better future, and represent possibility even when we are dragged.`：**三个并列谓语 + `even when` 让步状语从句**，用于展开梦想的三重作用；
> ③ 补充句② `Don't be pushed around by the fears in your mind. Be led by the dreams in your heart.`：**否定祈使句 + 肯定祈使句 + 被动结构 + 对照**，用于结尾给出行动号召。
> **附加结构钉子**：主句中 `that` 引导定语从句，修饰 `the ingredient`，`that` 在从句中作主语，谓语 `changes` 用单数；`change A to B` 中 `A = the hot water of mediocrity`，`B = the stream of success`。补充句①中三个谓语 `nourish / give / represent` 共用主语 `Dreams`；`give us a vision of a better future` 是“双宾语”结构。补充句②由两个祈使句构成，均为被动：`Don't be pushed around by...` 与 `Be led by...`；`fears in your mind` 对 `dreams in your heart`，`pushed around` 对 `led`，形成结构对称。
> **附加易错点**：引句用抽象名词 `Dream` 直接作主语，属于格言式表达；考场更稳妥的写法是 `A dream is...` 或 `Dreams are...`。`the ingredient` 中 `the` 不可省；`that changes` 不能写成 `who changes`，因为先行词是物。`mediocrity` 不可数，不加 `a`；`the stream of success` 中 `stream` 为可数名词单数。`nourish` 后直接接宾语，不写 `nourish for`；`give us a vision of...` 中 `of` 不可换成 `for`。`even when` 不写成 `even that`；`be dragged` 中 `dragged` 双写 `g`。`Don't be pushed around` 中 `pushed` 是过去分词；`Be led by` 中 `led` 不是 `lead`。

## ⭐ 主句（精读精析）

> **“Dream is the ingredient that changes the hot water of mediocrity to the stream of success.”**

**出处**：素材库 · 梦想 🌠 主题（议论体格言句；写“梦想 / 目标 / 坚持 / 理想与现实”类图画作文，可直接作第二段解释句或观点句）

**翻译**：梦想是把平庸的“热水”变成成功之溪的要素。／梦想能让平庸的处境汇成通往成功的溪流。

**结构拆解**：

- **骨架先化简为一条公式**：

  $$
  \underbrace{\text{Dream}}_{\text{主语}}\;
  \underbrace{\text{is}}_{\text{系动词}}\;
  \underbrace{\text{the ingredient}}_{\text{表语}}\;
  \underbrace{\text{that changes the hot water of mediocrity to the stream of success}}_{\text{定语从句，修饰 ingredient}}.
  $$

  全句主干是“主系表”：`Dream is the ingredient`。后面 `that...` 是定语从句，修饰 `the ingredient`；`that` 在从句中作主语，指代 `the ingredient`，所以谓语用单数 `changes`。

- **`that` 定语从句的作用**：
  - 没有从句时，`Dream is the ingredient` 只说了“梦想是要素”，信息太空。
  - `that changes...` 进一步说明“是什么要素”：它是“把平庸的热水变成成功之溪”的要素。
  - ⚠️ 先行词是物 `ingredient`，关系代词用 `that / which`，不能用 `who`。

- **`change A to B`：把 A 变成 B**：
  - `A = the hot water of mediocrity`（平庸的热水）
  - `B = the stream of success`（成功之溪）
  - `to` 表示转变的结果或方向。也可用 `change A into B`，但本句固定用 `to`，引用时不要改。
  - 考场可改写为：`turn A into B`，如 `Dreams turn the hot water of mediocrity into the stream of success.`

- **暗喻与对照**：
  - `the hot water of mediocrity` 把“平庸”写成“热水”，既可保留“水”的意象，也可联想习语 `be in hot water`（陷入困境）。
  - `the stream of success` 把“成功”写成“溪流”，与前面的“热水”形成水意象的延续。
  - `mediocrity`（平庸）与 `success`（成功）构成反义对照；`hot water` 与 `stream` 构成状态对比。
  - 这种写法比 `Dream is important` 更有画面感，也比空喊 `We should have dreams` 更适合议论文。

- **可迁移的等价改写（考场保底）**：
  - `A dream is the ingredient that changes the hot water of mediocrity to the stream of success.`（补冠词，最稳）
  - `Dreams are the ingredients that turn the hot water of mediocrity into the stream of success.`（复数泛指）
  - `It is dreams that change the hot water of mediocrity to the stream of success.`（强调句）
  - `Dream is what changes the hot water of mediocrity to the stream of success.`（`what` 从句作表语）
  - `Without dreams, we may remain in the hot water of mediocrity.`（反面假设，适合结尾）

- **语用价值**：本句把“梦想的作用”压缩成“要素 + 转变”的结构，既有抽象判断，又有比喻形象。图画作文若只写 `Dreams are important`，容易空泛；写出“梦想是改变平庸处境的关键要素”，第二段立刻具备“定义 + 比喻论证”的评分结构。

**词汇点**：

| 词汇 | 词性 / 释义 | 搭配与提示 |
|---|---|---|
| `dream` | n. 梦想；v. 做梦 | `dream of / about doing sth`；`dream up` 虚构 |
| `ingredient` | n. 要素；成分 | 可数名词；`the ingredient that...` |
| `change A to B` | 把 A 变成 B | 也可 `change A into B`；不写 `change A with B` |
| `hot water` | 热水；困境 | `be in hot water` 陷入麻烦 |
| `mediocrity` | n. 平庸 | 不可数；`the hot water of mediocrity` |
| `stream` | n. 溪流 | 可数；`the stream of success` |
| `success` | n. 成功 | 不可数偏多；`achieve success` |
| `nourish` | v. 滋养 | 及物；`nourish our spirit` |
| `spirit` | n. 精神 | 此处不可数；`our spirit` |
| `vision` | n. 愿景；视野 | `a vision of a better future` |
| `represent` | v. 代表；体现 | 及物；`represent possibility` |
| `possibility` | n. 可能性 | 抽象不可数；具体“一种可能”用 `a possibility` |
| `drag` | v. 拖；拖累 | 过去分词 `dragged`；`be dragged down by...` |
| `push around` | 随意摆布 | 被动 `be pushed around` |
| `lead` | v. 引领 | 过去式/过去分词 `led`；`be led by` |
| `fear` | n. 恐惧 | 可数/不可数；`fears in your mind` 种种恐惧 |

## 🔹 补充句 ①（作用展开）

> **“Dreams nourish our spirit, give us a vision of a better future, and represent possibility even when we are dragged.”**

**翻译**：梦想滋养我们的精神，给予我们更美好未来的愿景；即使我们被现实拖累，梦想依然代表着可能性。

**结构拆解**：

$$
\underbrace{\text{Dreams}}_{\text{主语}}\;
\underbrace{\text{nourish our spirit}}_{\text{谓语 1 + 宾语}}\;
\text{,}\;
\underbrace{\text{give us a vision of a better future}}_{\text{谓语 2 + 双宾语}}\;
\text{, and}\;
\underbrace{\text{represent possibility}}_{\text{谓语 3 + 宾语}}\;
\underbrace{\text{even when we are dragged}}_{\text{让步状语从句}}.
$$

- **三个并列谓语共用主语 `Dreams`**：
  - `nourish our spirit`：滋养精神
  - `give us a vision of a better future`：给我们更美好未来的愿景
  - `represent possibility`：代表可能性
  - 三个动作从“精神滋养”到“未来愿景”再到“可能性”，层层展开梦想的作用。

- **`give us a vision of...` 是双宾语结构**：
  - `give sb sth`：`us` 是间接宾语，`a vision` 是直接宾语。
  - `of a better future` 是介词短语，修饰 `a vision`。
  - ⚠️ 不写 `give us a vision for a better future` 更常见？这里 `of` 更稳，表示“关于……的愿景”。

- **`even when` 引导让步状语从句**：
  - `even when` = “即使当……的时候”，比 `when` 多一层让步。
  - `we are dragged` 是被动结构，省略了施动者；可补为 `we are dragged down by reality / by daily routines`。
  - `drag down` 更常见，意为“拖累、使消沉”；原句省略 `down` 也成立，但写作中补上更自然。

- **可迁移改写**：
  - `Dreams nourish our spirit, give us a vision of a better future, and represent possibility even when we are dragged down by reality.`（补 `down by reality`，更自然）
  - `Reading nourishes our spirit, gives us a vision of a better future, and represents possibility even when we are dragged down by daily routines.`（替换主语，注意三单）
  - `Friendship nourishes our spirit, gives us a vision of a better future, and represents possibility even when we are dragged down by difficulties.`

**词汇点**：

| 词汇 | 词性 / 释义 | 搭配与提示 |
|---|---|---|
| `nourish` | v. 滋养 | 及物；`nourish our spirit / mind` |
| `spirit` | n. 精神 | 此处不可数；`our spirit` |
| `give sb sth` | 给某人某物 | 双宾语；`give us a vision` |
| `vision` | n. 愿景 | `a vision of a better future` |
| `better future` | 更美好的未来 | `a better future` 可数单数 |
| `represent` | v. 代表 | 及物；`represent possibility` |
| `possibility` | n. 可能性 | 抽象不可数；`a possibility` 表“一种可能” |
| `even when` | 即使当…… | 引导让步状语从句 |
| `drag` | v. 拖累 | `be dragged down by...`；过去分词 `dragged` |

## 🔹 补充句 ②（行动号召）

> **“Don't be pushed around by the fears in your mind. Be led by the dreams in your heart.”**

**翻译**：不要被内心的恐惧随意摆布，要让心中的梦想引领你。

**结构拆解**：

$$
\underbrace{\text{Don't be pushed around}}_{\text{否定祈使 + 被动}}\;
\underbrace{\text{by the fears in your mind}}_{\text{by 短语表施动者}}.
\quad
\underbrace{\text{Be led}}_{\text{肯定祈使 + 被动}}\;
\underbrace{\text{by the dreams in your heart}}_{\text{by 短语表施动者}}.
$$

- **两个祈使句，一否定一肯定**：
  - `Don't be pushed around...`：不要被……摆布。
  - `Be led by...`：要被……引领。
  - 这是议论文结尾很常见的“先否定错误做法，再给出正确做法”结构。

- **被动语态 + `by` 短语**：
  - `be pushed around by...`：被……随意摆布；`push sb around` 是“摆布某人”。
  - `be led by...`：被……引领；`lead` 的过去分词是 `led`。
  - `by` 后面是动作的执行者：`the fears in your mind` / `the dreams in your heart`。

- **对照结构**：
  - `fears` 对 `dreams`：恐惧对梦想。
  - `in your mind` 对 `in your heart`：头脑中的担忧对心中的理想。
  - `pushed around` 对 `led`：被摆布对被引领。
  - 这种对称让句子有节奏感，适合做结尾金句。

- **可迁移改写**：
  - `Don't be pushed around by the fears in your mind; be led by the dreams in your heart.`（合为一句）
  - `Don't be dictated by temporary setbacks. Be guided by long-term goals.`（替换对照词）
  - `Don't be limited by the voices of doubt. Be driven by the light of your goal.`（更适合考研写作）

**词汇点**：

| 词汇 | 词性 / 释义 | 搭配与提示 |
|---|---|---|
| `push around` | 随意摆布 | 被动 `be pushed around` |
| `fear` | n. 恐惧 | `fears in your mind` 种种恐惧 |
| `mind` | n. 头脑；心智 | `in your mind` |
| `lead` | v. 引领 | 过去分词 `led`；`be led by` |
| `heart` | n. 内心；情感 | `in your heart` |
| `dictate` | v. 支配；决定 | `be dictated by...` 可替换 `be pushed around by` |
| `guide` | v. 引导 | `be guided by...` 可替换 `be led by` |

## ✍️ 仿写迁移（直接套用）

| 模板 | 适用场景 | 示例 |
|---|---|---|
| `X is the ingredient that changes the hot water of mediocrity to the stream of success.` | 说明某品质/因素的关键作用 | `Perseverance is the ingredient that changes the hot water of mediocrity to the stream of success.` |
| `X nourish(es) our spirit, give(s) us a vision of a better future, and represent(s) possibility even when we are dragged down by reality.` | 展开某事物的三重作用 | `Reading nourishes our spirit, gives us a vision of a better future, and represents possibility even when we are dragged down by daily routines.` |
| `Don't be pushed around by the fears in your mind. Be led by the dreams in your heart.` | 结尾号召 | `Don't be pushed around by the fears in your mind. Be led by the dreams in your heart.` |
| `Don't be dictated by temporary setbacks. Be guided by long-term goals.` | 考研/学习类结尾 | `Don't be dictated by temporary setbacks. Be guided by long-term goals.` |

**替换词库**：

- `ingredient` → `driving force / key / catalyst`（注意可数性与冠词）
- `the hot water of mediocrity` → `the swamp of routine / the comfort zone / the trap of mediocrity`
- `the stream of success` → `the road to success / the river of achievement`
- `nourish our spirit` → `enrich our mind / strengthen our will`
- `a vision of a better future` → `a clear picture of a better future / a sense of direction`
- `represent possibility` → `keep hope alive / open up possibilities`
- `be pushed around by` → `be dictated by / be controlled by`
- `be led by` → `be guided by / be driven by`

**考场段落示例**：

> A dream is not a castle in the air; it is the ingredient that changes the hot water of mediocrity to the stream of success. Dreams nourish our spirit, give us a vision of a better future, and represent possibility even when we are dragged down by reality. Therefore, we should not be pushed around by the fears in our mind; instead, we should be led by the dreams in our heart.
