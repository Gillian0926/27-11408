# ✍️ 英语作文技巧 06 · 图表作文模板（英语二大作文）

> ⚠️ **英语二大作文 = 图表作文**（柱状图 / 饼图 / 折线图 / 表格），15 分，**150 词以上**。
> 和英语一的图画作文**不是一回事**：不需要"寓意升华、精神品质"那套，核心是 **读图 → 描述数据 → 分析原因 → 预测/建议**。
> 结构分照拿：**三段式 + 数据准确 + 趋势词到位**。

## 一、万能三段式

### 第一段：描述图表（2-3 句，约 50 词）
1. **总体句**：图表类型 + 主题 + 时间范围
2. **趋势句**：最主要的变化 / 对比（用具体数字）
3. **补充句**（可选）：另一个突出细节

**模板句**：
- As is vividly illustrated in the **bar chart**, the figure of ____ **rose steadily** from A in 20xx to B in 20xx.
- The **pie chart** clearly shows the **proportion** of ____, which **accounts for** nearly ____%.
- As can be seen from the **table**, there was a **sharp increase / a slight drop** in ____ during the period.

### 第二段：分析原因（3-4 句，约 70 词）——**拿分核心**
1. 点题：This **noticeable change / striking contrast** can be attributed to several factors.
2. 原因一：**To begin with**, ...（经济发展 / 政策 / 观念变化）
3. 原因二：**Moreover / In addition**, ...
4. 举例或递进：**A case in point is** ... / **Another factor worth mentioning is** ...

**万能原因库**：经济发展与收入提高 / 科技进步与互联网普及 / 政策支持与观念转变 / 生活水平与消费升级 / 教育水平提升。

### 第三段：结论 / 预测 / 建议（2-3 句，约 40 词）
1. 结论：**From the data above, we can draw the conclusion that** ...
2. 预测：If this trend continues, ____ will ____ in the years to come.
3. 建议/展望：**Accordingly, it is advisable for us to** ... / **Only in this way can we** ...

## 二、高分套路示范

**题目**：某市居民出行方式占比，2010→2020 年公共自行车由 5% 升至 25%，私家车由 60% 降至 45%。

> As is vividly illustrated in the **two pie charts**, the **proportion** of public bicycles in local residents' travel **climbed markedly** from 5% in 2010 to 25% in 2020, while that of private cars **dropped** from 60% to 45%. Such a **noticeable shift** can be attributed to several factors. **To begin with**, the government has invested heavily in shared-bike systems and green transport. **Moreover**, growing environmental awareness has led more people to choose low-carbon travel. **A case in point is** that many commuters now ride to the subway instead of driving. **From the data above, we can draw the conclusion that** public transport is playing an increasingly important role. If this trend continues, our city will become greener and less congested in the years to come.

## 三、易错点 ⚠️

1. **别写成图画作文**：不要"the picture conveys a profound truth about life"这类，图表要**就数据说数据**。
2. **数据要准**：抄错数字、把升降说反，直接扣分。上升/下降、快/慢，动词别混。
3. **时态**：图表描述一般用**过去时**（有时间范围）或**现在时**（现状类）；预测用**将来时**。
4. **别堆数据**：挑**最大变化、最重要对比**，1-2 组数字足够，其余概括。
5. 字数 ≥150，写 **160-180** 最稳。
