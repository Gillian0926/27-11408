# ✍️ 仿写句式库（爪爪整理版）

> 核心思维：**补充说明**——主句放主要信息，次要信息用高级结构补充。
> 效果：信息密度高、简洁地道，比"两个短句"高级一个档次。
> 用法：每个句式背 1 个例句 + 结构公式，写作时套用。

---

## 一、分词状语补充说明（最常用）

**结构**：`主句（概括），v-ing / v-ed 分词短语（具体化）`

**规则**：分词动作必须由主句主语发出；主动 v-ing，被动 v-ed；无时态概念。

**场景**：趋势/规律放主句，具体数据、表现放状语。

**例句**：
- Online education is booming in China, **attracting tens of millions of learners**.
- Student enrolment kept rising from 1999 to 2011, **before falling by 2% in 2012**.
- 主句摆态度 + 分词给观点：Some experts are optimistic about AI, **believing it will create more jobs than it destroys**.

**仿写**：写"我国新能源汽车销量大涨，去年同比增长 40%"
→ *Sales of new-energy vehicles are surging in China, growing by 40% last year.*

---

## 二、with 复合结构（主语可不同）

**结构**：`with + 名词 + 分词 / 形容词 / 介词短语`

**规则**：逻辑主语可以和主句主语**不同**（比分词状语灵活）；相当于省略 be 的主系表。

**场景**：具体表现、举例、当前趋势；表原因时放句首。

**例句**：
- Several cities saw a surge in travel, **with people queuing for hours at scenic spots**.
- **With schools shut**, many parents had to juggle work and childcare. （原因，放句首）
- China is the world's largest emitter, **with coal powering most of its grid**.

**仿写**：写"考研人数屡创新高，其中许多是二战考生"
→ *The number of postgraduate applicants keeps hitting records, with many of them retaking the exam.*

---

## 三、分词/形容词短语作原因状语（= because 从句）

**结构**：`Lacking / Faced with / Frustrated by / Keen for ..., 主句`

**规则**：逻辑主语 = 主句主语；放句首，表示"由于……"。

**例句**：
- **Lacking access to the internet**, rural students may fall behind their peers.
- **Faced with fierce competition**, graduates are eager to acquire practical skills.
- **Frustrated by slow progress**, he decided to change his study method.

**仿写**：写"由于缺乏经验，许多毕业生找工作困难"
→ *Lacking work experience, many graduates find it hard to land a job.*

---

## 四、状语从句简化（省略主语 + be）

**结构**：`when / while / if / unless / once / although + v-ing / v-ed / 形容词`

**规则**：从句主语 = 主句主语，且从句含 be 动词时，可省略"主语 + be"。

**例句**：
- AI, **when used responsibly**, can augment human creativity.
- The cost, **though not small**, is still affordable for most families.
- **If left untreated**, hearing problems can affect one's whole life.

**仿写**：写"如果合理规划，碎片时间也能高效利用"
→ *If properly planned, fragmented time can also be used efficiently.*

---

## 五、非限定从 / 同位语补背景（次要信息不抢戏）

**结构 A**：`名词, which ...`（补背景/次要原因/今昔对比）
**结构 B**：`名词, 概括词 + 后置定语`（同位语）

**例句**：
- Remote working, **which was rare before the pandemic**, has gone mainstream. （今昔对比）
- Knowledge, **which used to be reserved for the elite**, is now free for everyone.
- Thailand lifted visa requirements for Indians, **a privilege usually reserved for rich countries**. （同位语）
- The exam is getting harder, **a trend that shows no sign of slowing**. （同位语修饰整句）

**仿写**：写"杭州以西湖闻名，每年吸引数百万游客"
→ *Hangzhou, famous for its West Lake, attracts millions of tourists every year.*

---

## 六、避免重复（one/ones、that/those、so 倒装）

| 结构 | 用途 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| one / ones | 泛指同类（可数） | Old skills fade, but **new ones** are emerging. |
| that / those | 特指（A 的 the X of B） | The number of applicants has doubled **that of** last year. |
| so + 助动词 + 主语 | 避免谓语重复 | The industry is booming, and **so are** related businesses. |

**例句**：
- A degree in computer science is more lucrative than **one in literature**.
- As their income has grown, **so has** their standard of living.

**仿写**：写"房价在涨，租金也在涨"
→ *House prices are rising, and so are rents.*

---

## 七、否定词 + 比较结构表强调（开头神器）

**结构**：`No / Few / Never + 比较级 / as...as...`

**场景**：文章开头引出主题、强调"最……"。

**例句**：
- **No** factor is **more crucial to** success **than** perseverance.
- **Few** places illustrate the digital divide **as clearly as** rural China.
- There has **never been** a **more** demanding era **than** the age of AI.

**仿写**：写"没有比教育更能改变命运的了"
→ *No force transforms a person's fate more powerfully than education.*

---

## 八、强调句（It is...that...）

**结构**：`It is / was + 被强调部分 + that / who + 其余`

**变体**：It was **not until**...that...；It is **not only**...that...；**not...but...**；**rather than**

**例句**：
- **It is** persistence, **not** talent, **that** leads to success.
- **It was not until** he failed **that** he realized the importance of planning.
- **It is not just** money **that** can be stolen; so too can privacy.

**仿写**：写"正是自律，而不是天赋，决定了你走多远"
→ *It is self-discipline, rather than talent, that determines how far you can go.*

---

## 九、as much A as B / not so much A as B

**结构**：
- `as much A as B`：既是 A 也是 B
- `not so much A as B`：与其说 A，不如说 B

**例句**：
- For students, the exam is **as much** a test of willpower **as** of knowledge.
- The problem is **not so much** a lack of time **as** a lack of focus.

**仿写**：写"考研与其说是考知识，不如说是考心态"
→ *The exam is not so much a test of knowledge as of mentality.*

---

## 十、the more...the more...（因果递增）

**结构**：`The + 比较级（从句），the + 比较级（主句）`

**变体**：搭配 It is adj to do / There be。

**例句**：
- **The more** you practise, **the more** confident you become.
- **The longer** you procrastinate, **the harder** it is to start.

**仿写**：写"你积累的越多，考试时就越从容"
→ *The more you accumulate, the calmer you will be in the exam.*

---

## 十一、进阶：Just as...so... / 独立主格 / be it...or...

**Just as...so...（类比）**：
- *Just as plants need sunlight, so do students need encouragement.*

**独立主格（补充具体信息）**：
- *The crisis has had three consequences, most of them negative.*
- *Coal kills millions every year, most of them in poor countries.*

**be it...or...（无论……还是……）**：
- *Mastering any skill, be it playing an instrument or coding, requires patience.*

---

## 📋 应用速查：段落里怎么用

| 位置 | 推荐句式 |
|---|---|
| 开头段（描述） | 分词状语、with 结构（给数据细节） |
| 开头段（引出话题） | 否定+比较、强调句 |
| 分析段（原因） | 分词原因状语、非限定从（次要原因） |
| 分析段（举例） | with 结构、独立主格 |
| 对比（今昔/反差） | 非限定从 which used to、so 倒装 |
| 结尾段（总结） | 强调句、the more...the more...、倒装收尾 |
