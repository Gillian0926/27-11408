# 📖 英语作文素材句库（15 大主题 · 每日句子素材源）

> **来源**：思岐的考研英语学习资料（图画作文主题词素材），爪爪整理归档
> **用途**：每日精读句子的挑选素材库；每个主题背 3 句足够，应用时不限主题
> **用法**：爪爪每天从这里挑 1-3 句做"每日精读"；你自己也可以按主题查句

---

## 1. 梦想 🌠

**主题词**：beautiful fantasies / bubble / castle in the air / the fabric of dream / what drives us forward

- Dream is the ingredient that changes the hot water of mediocrity to the stream of success.
  梦想是要素，可以让平庸的热水淌成溪流，通向成功。
- Dreams nourish our spirit, give us a vision of a better future, and represent possibility even when we are dragged.
  梦想滋养灵魂，让我们对未来拥有更美好的期待。即使为现实所累，梦想依然带来希望。
- Don't be pushed around by the fears in your mind. Be led by the dreams in your heart.
  不要被你内心的恐惧所左右，要勇于追逐心中的梦想。

## 2. 乐观积极 ☀️

**主题词**：optimism / positive thinking / bright/rosy outlook / hopefulness / what gives us enthusiasm

- Instead of worrying about what you cannot control, shift your energy to what you can create.
  与其杞人忧天，不如专注当下。
- Storms always give way to the sun. Winter always thaws into spring time.
  风雨过后见阳光，冬去春必来。
- Don't dream up thousands of reasons why you can't do what you want to; find one reason why you can.
  与其找寻千万个借口去逃避，不如找寻一个理由去面对。
- There should be laughter after pain. There should be sunshine after rain.
  痛后是欢笑，雨后见彩虹。

## 3. 消极否定 🌧️

**主题词**：pessimism / despair / negative thinking / gloomy outlook / what drags us down

- Such negative reactions only produce greater problems by promising a temporary "solution" to the pressing problem.
  消极应对，可以临时"解决"棘手的问题，但却会产生更大的问题。
- To live in fear is like being in an emotional prison. Fear destroys our potential and ability.
  恐惧禁锢着我们的情感，破坏了我们的潜能和人际关系、身体健康。
- Many people walk around with a meaningless life.
  许多人像行尸走肉般过着了无意义的生活。

## 4. 珍惜时间 ⏰

**主题词**：the value of time / valuable, productive time / precious hours / what yields fruits

- If you gain control of your lifetime, you will gain control of your life.
  掌控你的一分一秒，就能掌控你的人生。
- The days flow away, and I do feel my hands are getting empty.
  日过匆匆，我的手确乎是渐渐空虚了。
- There are only two creatures who can surmount the pyramids—the eagle and the snail.
  只有两种生物能登上金字塔的顶端——苍鹰和蜗牛。（坚持者胜）

## 5. 学习教育 📚

**主题词**：a willingness to learn / the theory, purpose, fabric of education / what lights a fire

- What we know is nothing; what we don't know is immense.
  我所知，沧海一粟；我所不知，无边无涯。
- Education is not the filling of a pail, but the lighting of a fire.
  教育不是装满一桶水，而是点燃一团火。
- I wish that education does not just give me something to drink, but makes me feel thirsty.
  我希望，教育不仅提供饮水，还能够激发我的渴求。
- Don't go through life. Grow through life.
  不要做生活的过客，而是要不断成长。

## 6. 人生意义 🌍

**主题词**：our existence / meaningful, fulfilling life / the theory, purpose, fabric of life

- Life is about accepting the challenges along the way, choosing to keep moving forward, and savoring the journey.
  生活就是，一路接受挑战，一路勇往直前，一路耐人寻味。
- Living without an aim is like sailing without a compass.
  生活没有目标，犹如航海没有罗盘。
- The way you get meaning into your life is to devote yourself to loving others, and devote yourself to creating something that gives you purpose and meaning.
  为人生赋予意义的方式：全情投入，爱他人，去创造，获取目标与价值。

## 7. 奋斗 💪

**主题词**：endeavor / keep going / fighting for your life / what yields fruits

- Let us not always rest our eyes on what we did yesterday, but let us direct our attention to what we are going to do tomorrow.
  我们的目光不要总是停留在过去，而是要关注未来。
- Success is a journey, not a destination.
  成功是一段旅程，而不是终点。
- Fighting for life is such a wonderful experience, full of every possibility.
  生而奋斗，是一种美妙的经历，充满了无尽的可能性。

## 8. 感恩 🙏

**主题词**：gratitude / appreciation / thankfulness / being grateful, thankful

- Gratitude is a beautiful word.
  感激，是一个美好的词语。
- Gratitude teaches us the art of cooperation and communication.
  感激，传递给我们合作与沟通的艺术。
- Gratitude can mean a smile, or a thank-you, or a gesture of appreciation.
  感激，可以是一个微笑、一句谢谢，也可以是一个感动之举。

## 9. 亲情 ❤️

**主题词**：motherly/fatherly love / family love, affection / everlasting love

- Motherly love by its very nature is unconditional.
  在本质上，母爱是无条件付出。
- Family love is such a steady and joyful light in my life.
  亲情是生活中的一道光，稳稳的，令人愉悦。
- Your family lifts you up in spirits and makes that dark and empty world seem bright and full.
  亲情让你振作起来，使你空虚而黑暗的世界变得明亮和充实。

## 10. 友情 🤝

**主题词**：the theory, fabric, ingredients of friendship / closeness / intimacy

- Half the fun of doing anything is sharing it with others.
  与他人分享，快乐增倍。
- Friends allow us to savor our success and our joys, comfort us in our challenging moments, and provide a mirror for us to learn more about ourselves.
  朋友使我们得以品味成功和快乐，在艰难时刻得到抚慰；他们就像一面镜子，让我们对自己有了更多的了解。
- The support of a friend during a tough time could make the difference between success and failure.
  在艰难时刻，朋友的支持可以决定你的成败。
- Friend, a word so small, yet so large in feeling.
  朋友，字虽小，情却深。

## 11. 人与人的关爱 💗

**主题词**：love of mankind / charity / philanthropy / caring for others / benevolence

- Love shelters you from the rain. Without love, life would be a mistake.
  爱为你遮风挡雨。没有爱，人生将是一个错误。
- If there is light in the soul, there will be beauty in the person.
  灵魂中有光，人就会变美。
- Care for others the way you would like them to care for you.
  你希望别人怎样关心你，你就怎样关心别人。
- Encouraging words said to people in despair can inspire them with renewed spirit.
  给绝望中的人几句鼓励，可以使他们重新振奋起来。

## 12. 融合与差异 🌈

**主题词**：harmony / difference / cooperation / collaboration / what combines us

- Difference does not mean inferior or superior. It is the difference that keeps each culture what it is.
  差异并不意味着低人一等；正是这种差异使每种文化具有其独特性。
- Without difference, our world will be a garden with few flowers and a library with few books.
  没有了差异，我们的世界将成为一个没有几朵花的花园、一座没有几本书的图书馆。
- An argument tries to prove who is right, whereas a discussion tries to prove what is right.
  争论是为了证明谁是对的，而讨论是为了证明什么是对的。

## 13. 态度抉择 🧭

**主题词**：viewpoint / judgment / choice / stance / angle / perspective

- Your living is determined not so much by what life brings you as by the attitude you bring to life.
  你的人生，与其说取决于生活怎样对待你，不如说取决于你对待生活的态度。
- We can't always choose our circumstances. But we can choose our attitude.
  我们无法选择自己的境况，但是我们可以决定自己的态度。
- You don't drown by falling in the water. You drown by staying there.
  掉进水里不会淹死，呆在那里才会溺水而亡。

## 14. 科技 🤖

**主题词**：AI / cybersecurity / virtual reality

- AI technology is transforming industries like healthcare and education, ultimately improving efficiency and decision-making.
  AI 技术正在改变医疗和教育等行业，并最终提高效率和决策能力。
- AI-driven educational tools can provide personalized learning experiences, enhancing education's effectiveness.
  人工智能驱动的教学工具可以提供个性化的学习体验，从而提高教学效果。
- With the increasing reliance on digital technologies, cybersecurity measures are crucial to protect personal data, ensuring a safe online environment.
  随着对数字技术的日益依赖，网络安全措施对于保护个人数据至关重要，其可确保上网环境的安全性。
- VR technology immerses users in simulated environments, making learning and entertainment more interactive.
  VR 技术使用户沉浸在模拟环境当中，这让学习和娱乐更具交互性。

## 15. 环境 🌿

**主题词**：climate change / renewable energy / environmental education

- Climate change poses a grave threat to our planet, causing rising temperatures, extreme weather events, and sea-level rise. Mitigation and adaptation strategies are essential to combat its effects.
  气候变化对地球构成了严重威胁，其导致气温上升、极端天气事件增多和海平面上升。减缓和适应策略至关重要。
- Transitioning to renewable energy sources like solar and wind power can reduce greenhouse gas emissions, combat air pollution, and promote a sustainable energy future.
  转而采用太阳能和风能等可再生能源可以减少温室气体排放，对抗空气污染，并促进可持续能源的发展。
- Educating the public about environmental issues and sustainable practices is crucial for fostering a sense of responsibility and promoting eco-friendly behaviors.
  就环保和可持续发展事宜对公众进行教育，这对于培养责任感和敦促环保行为至关重要。

## 🏋️ 使用建议

- **背诵节奏**：每天背 1 个主题的 3 句（约 5 分钟），15 天一轮，考前循环 2-3 轮
- **万能用法**：素材句不限主题，描述现象/原因/建议段都能套（如"梦想"句可用于奋斗类话题）
- **爪爪配合**：每天"每日精读"优先从这个库里挑句，配难词/短语拆解
