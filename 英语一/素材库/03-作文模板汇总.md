# 📖 英语作文模板汇总（图画 + 材料作文）

> **来源**：思岐的考研英语资料（图画作文模板、材料作文模板、免费+付费模板结合应用），爪爪整理
> **用途**：考场直接套用的三段式框架。先背框架，再换主题词。

---

## 🖼️ 图画作文模板（英一主考题型，160-200 词）

### 第一段：描述图画（3-4 句）

**开篇引言**（选 1 句）：
- The picture/cartoon appears to offer a vivid glimpse into ...
- As is vividly shown in the picture, ...
- The drawing portrays a thought-provoking scene in which ...

**细节描述**：
- In the center of the picture, ...
- What draws our attention is that ...

**图注/象征**：
- The caption reads, "..."
- What the picture intends to convey is that ...（点题过渡句）

### 第二段：分析寓意（5-6 句）

**点题**：The picture is intended to remind us of the importance of ...（主题词）

**分析**（选 2 种方式）：
- 社会现象类：This phenomenon can be attributed to ...
- 寓意哲理类：... plays an indispensable role in our life
- 行为行事类：It is advisable that we should ...

**举例**：For instance, ... / A case in point is ...

### 第三段：评论建议（2-3 句）

- In my view, ... / From my perspective, ...
- It is high time that we took action to ...
- Only in this way can we ...（倒装收尾）

---

## 📄 材料作文模板（英一新题型，160-200 词）

> 大纲新增题型：给一段 100 字左右的短文，问是否同意/在何种程度同意作者观点，用推理和举例支持。

### 开头段（表态 2-3 句）
- The passage argues that ...
- I largely agree with the author's view for the following reasons. / I find myself in partial agreement with ...

### 中间段（论证 4-5 句）
- First and foremost, ...（理由 1 + 解释）
- Moreover, ...（理由 2 + 例证）
- A case in point is ...（举例支撑）

### 结尾段（总结 2 句）
- In conclusion, I firmly believe that ... because ...
- Therefore, it is essential that we ...（建议/展望）

## 🧱 免费模板 × 付费模板结合（句式扩展）

**开头段扩展**：
- 免费版：As is vividly shown in the picture, ...
- 升级版：The picture appears to offer a vivid glimpse into + 主题词，which reminds us that ...

**中间段扩展**：
- 社会现象式：In contemporary society, + 现象 + has become increasingly common.
- 寓意哲理式：Just as the picture conveys, + 道理 + is the very foundation of ...
- 行为行事式：When faced with + 情境, we should + 建议行为.

**结尾段扩展**：
- 展望式：Only by doing ... can we look forward to a brighter future.
- 呼吁式：It is high time that both individuals and society took ... seriously.

---

## ⚠️ 使用提醒

1. **模板是骨架，主题词是肉**：背熟框架 + 主题词表，考场 10 分钟写出第一段
2. **别整段套话**：模板句之间要填充具体内容（图画细节、自己的理由）
3. **材料作文**：表态必须明确（同意/部分同意/反对），理由要有层次
4. 更多范文见《04-范文导览》
