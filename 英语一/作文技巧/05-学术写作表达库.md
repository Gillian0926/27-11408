# ✍️ 学术写作表达库（Phrasebank 精华 · 爪爪筛选版）

> **来源**：The Academic Phrasebank, The University of Manchester（曼彻斯特大学学术写作语料库）
> **用途**：考研英语图表/图画作文 + 论证段落的高级表达。爪爪筛选出考研适用的部分，按写作功能分类。
> **用法**：背熟每类 2-3 个，写作时替换关键词套用。

---

## 📈 描述趋势（图表作文核心！）

**描述变化（The graph shows...）**：
- The graph shows that there has been a **steady / sharp / gradual / marked** rise in the number of ...
- Figure 2 reveals that there has been a **dramatic / slight** fall in ...

**突出亮点（What stands out...）**：
- What **stands out** in this chart is the **rapid growth** of ...
- What is **striking** is the **steady decline** of ...
- What can be **clearly seen** is the **difference** between ...
- The most noticeable trend is the **phenomenal growth** of ...

**高低点（peaks and troughs）**：
- Production of X **peaked** in 1985.
- The number of X **reached a peak** during ...
- The rate **fell to a low point** of 5% at the end of the year.

**预测（projections）**：
- The rate is **likely to / expected to / projected to** level off after 2025.
- The number is **expected to rise to** 10 million by 2030.
- The amount is likely to **remain steady / decline steadily / drop sharply**.

**趋势词速查**：rise/increase/grow（升）、fall/decline/drop（降）、level off（趋平）、peak（达峰）、fluctuate（波动）、double/triple（翻倍/三倍）

## 🎯 引入话题（开头段）

**建立背景/重要性**：
- Over the past decade, there has been a growing concern about ...
- In recent years, ... has become an increasingly important issue.
- The past few years have witnessed a dramatic change in ...

**引出话题/问题**：
- It is widely acknowledged that ...
- There is a growing tendency for people to ...
- The picture vividly depicts a common phenomenon: ...

**定义/点题**：
- By ..., I mean ...
- What the picture intends to convey is that ...

## ⚖️ 对比对照（分析段）

- In contrast / By contrast, ...
- Compared with A, B ...
- While A tends to ..., B, on the other hand, ...
- A, whereas B ...
- There is a striking contrast between ... and ...
- 程度差异：A is **more ... than** B / A is **as ... as** B / A differs **markedly** from B

## 🔍 解释原因（分析段）

- The reasons for this are manifold.
- This is largely due to ...
- Another explanation is that ...

## 💡 举例论证

## 🏁 总结收尾（结尾段）

- In conclusion / To sum up / On the whole, ...
- The evidence suggests that ...
- It is therefore essential that ...
- Only in this way can we ...
- The findings imply that ... / This has important implications for ...

---

## ⚠️ 使用提醒

1. **别整段套模板**：这些是"短语积木"，每个功能类挑 1-2 个穿插进自己的句子，不要堆砌
2. **关键词替换**：把 number/rate/production 换成你的话题词（如 the number of postgraduate applicants）
3. 图表作文必背：**描述趋势 4 类 + 高低点 + 预测**，考场上直接默写
4. 论证段最常用：**因果 + 对比 + 举例**，各背 3 个就够

> 配合《04-仿写句式库》使用效果更佳：句式库管"句子怎么造高级"，表达库管"内容怎么组织"。
