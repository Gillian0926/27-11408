# ✍️ 英语作文技巧 01 · 大作文三段式框架

> 英语一大作文（图画作文为主 + 图表作文），20 分。**结构分是白送的，先搭骨架再填肉。**

## 📐 经典三段式

### 第一段：描述（2-3 句，约 60 词）
1. 总体描述：The picture vividly depicts...（图画）/ The chart shows...（图表）
2. 细节描述：In the center, ... / As is shown, ...（挑 1-2 个关键细节）
3. 图注/象征：The caption reads... / What the picture conveys is that...

**模板句**：
- As is vividly shown in the picture, ...
- The drawing portrays a thought-provoking scene in which ...
- 图表：As is clearly illustrated in the chart, the figure of X increased dramatically from A to B during the period.

### 第二段：分析（4-5 句，约 100 词）—— 拿分核心
1. 点题：What the picture intends to convey is that ...（主题句）
2. 原因 1：To begin with, ...（现象背后原因）
3. 原因 2：Moreover, ... / Besides, ...
4. 举例/论证：For instance, ... / A case in point is ...

**万能主题句**：
- 社会现象：This phenomenon can be attributed to several factors.
- 精神品质：This quality plays an indispensable role in our life.
- 科技：The rapid advancement of technology has brought about profound changes.

### 第三段：评论/建议（2-3 句，约 50 词）
1. 表态：In my view, ... / From my perspective, ...
2. 建议：It is advisable that we ... / Measures should be taken to ...
3. 展望：Only in this way can we ...（倒装收尾，高级！）

**收尾万能句**：
- Only in this way can we embrace a brighter future.
- It is high time that we took immediate action.

## ⚠️ 常见扣分点
1. **跑题**：第二段主题句必须扣住图画寓意，别写泛
2. **字数不足**：160-200 词区间（写 180 左右最稳）
3. **模板痕迹过重**：模板句之间要穿插具体内容，别整段套话
4. **语法硬伤**：单复数、时态一致性是改卷第一眼看到的
