# ✍️ 英语作文技巧 02 · 小作文模板（书信/通知）

> 英语一小作文 10 分：书信（建议信/邀请信/投诉信/申请信/道歉信）+ 通知。**格式分白送，背模板稳拿 6+。**

## 📮 书信通用框架

**称呼**：
- 不熟的人：Dear Sir or Madam,
- 熟人/老师：Dear Professor/Dr./Mr. Wang,

**开头（表明写信目的，1 句）**：
- 建议信：I am writing to put forward some suggestions concerning...
- 邀请信：I am writing to invite you to...
- 投诉信：I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with...
- 申请信：I am writing to apply for the position of...
- 道歉信：I am writing to apologize for...

**正文（2-3 点内容，分条写清楚）**：
- First and foremost, ... / Additionally, ... / Last but not least, ...

**结尾（礼貌收束）**：
- I would appreciate it if you could take my suggestions into consideration.
- I am looking forward to your reply at your earliest convenience.
- Thank you for your time and consideration.

**落款**：Yours sincerely, / Yours faithfully,  → 下一行 Li Ming（考研默认署名 Li Ming）

## 📢 通知框架（Notice）

**标题**：Notice（居中）
**正文**：
- 事由 + 时间地点 + 注意事项
- The Students' Union is organizing... which will be held in... on...
- All students are warmly welcome to participate.
- Anyone interested may sign up before...
**落款**：The Students' Union  → 日期（右对齐）

## ⚠️ 易错点
1. **格式错误扣分狠**：称呼、落款、日期位置都要对
2. 投诉信别骂人，用 I must complain about 而不是 angry 词汇
3. 通知用第三人称（students are welcome），书信用 I/you
4. 字数 100 词左右，别写太长
